The problem

Commerce went global. Payments didn't.

Talent, trade and customers now cross borders every day, but money still runs into currencies, intermediaries and incompatible systems. For people and businesses in emerging markets, the friction is even greater.

01

Fragmented payment systems

Banks, payment platforms and digital asset networks rarely connect to each other.

02

Expensive international transfers

Layers of intermediaries and margins make sending money across borders costly.

03

Unclear conversion costs

Hidden spreads and unclear fees make it hard to know what the recipient will actually get.

04

Settlement delays

Funds can take days to arrive, holding up businesses and families who need them now.

05

Hard to connect financial systems

With about 180 fiat currencies and thousands of digital assets, there is no unified layer joining them.

The Midas approach

One simple flow, from funding to delivery.

Midas is designed to bridge traditional finance, foreign exchange markets and digital assets, so the sender picks what to pay with and the recipient gets what they need.

  1. 1

    Choose currency and amount

    Select the currency you want to pay with and the amount you want to send.

  2. 2

    Review rate, fees and amount received

    See the exchange rate, the fee and exactly what the recipient will receive, before you commit.

  3. 3

    Confirm and track

    Confirm the payment and follow its status from submission to completion.

Our product goals

  • Transparent pricing with competitive FX and digital asset conversion rates
  • Fast, secure settlement across currencies
  • Interoperability across banks, payment platforms and digital asset networks
  • A scalable API layer for businesses, merchants and financial institutions

These are goals for the platform we are building. Capabilities will be announced as they are launched and verified.

Why Midas

Remove currency as a barrier to global commerce.

Information has become global. Talent has become global. Commerce has become global. Yet payments remain constrained by borders, currencies and fragmented financial systems.

Midas begins in Africa and expands worldwide, with a focus on emerging markets and on the individuals and small businesses that traditional finance often underserves.

Vision

To build the world's most intelligent and trusted payment infrastructure, enabling the seamless movement of value across currencies, borders and financial systems.

Mission

To accelerate the global economy with a secure, borderless payment platform that enables multi-currency transactions, advances digital commerce, supports international trade and drives financial inclusion.
